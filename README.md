# Orion

Rehearse a change to your company in a parallel universe before you make it.

Orion keeps a company's people, teams, services, decisions and conversations in one FalkorDB graph. An agent
can fork that graph, apply a change (retire a service, someone leaves, two teams merge), and show what would
break, who must approve, and where every claim came from.

Work in progress for Graph Hacks (FalkorDB × WeMakeDevs).
